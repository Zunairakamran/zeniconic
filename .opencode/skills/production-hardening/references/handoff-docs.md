# Handoff docs

The user is a beginner, in a hurry, and has never opened a video editor. Every
document here is written in the second person, names the button they can see, and
stops before it needs a glossary.

Four artefacts ship together: a one-page guide, eight troubleshooting entries, a
five-minute recording script and a printed card. They live in the app under
**Help → Guides**, as PDFs in `docs/handoff/`, and the card goes in the box.

## Writing rules

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Second person, sentence case | "Choose where the video is going." |
| Name the button exactly | Click **Export**, not "submit the render" |
| One idea per sentence, ≤18 words | "The app copies your clips into the project folder." |
| Numbers as digits | "3 minutes", "20 seconds" |
| No jargon | "video", not "render"; "version", not "variant"; "clips", not "assets" |
| Errors say what, why, next | "There isn't enough space. Free 5 GB, then click Try again." |
| No exclamation marks, no "simply" | — |
| Reading ease ≥60 (Flesch) | Check before publishing |

A sentence that needs a second read is a sentence to rewrite. If a screenshot
needs an arrow to be understood, the UI is wrong, not the screenshot.

## One page: make your first video

Print at A4, 12 pt, 1.4 line height, three screenshots on the right. Word count
247.

> **Make your first video**
>
> You need: a folder of clips from the property, the price, the number of
> bedrooms and bathrooms, and your model key.
>
> 1. Open Zeniconic. The first time, paste your model key when asked and click
>    **Check key**. You will see that it works, and your credit if the key has a
>    limit.
> 2. Click **New listing**. Type the address, then drag the folder of clips onto
>    the window. The app copies them into the project folder. Your originals are
>    not changed.
> 3. Fill in the price, bedrooms, bathrooms and address. Tick **These are
>    correct**. The app will not put a fact on screen until you do.
> 4. Click **Check clips**. A 10-clip listing takes about 3 minutes on a laptop.
>    You can keep working while it runs.
> 5. Pick a version. If you are unsure, start with **Just listed**. Click **Make
>    video**.
> 6. Watch the preview. To change something, type it in the chat, for example
>    "make it 20 seconds" or "start with the garden". Click **Apply** on the card
>    to keep the change.
> 7. Choose where the video is going: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or the MLS.
>    Click **Export**. The files land in **Documents → Zeniconic → your
>    address → deliveries**.
>
> If the preview looks wrong, nothing is broken. Press **Ctrl+Z** or click
> **Undo**. Your last good version is always kept.
>
> Stuck? Click **? → Copy diagnostics**, then send it to support@zeniconic.app.
> It contains no videos and no personal details.

The three screenshots: the key dialog with the status line; the version picker with **Just listed** selected; the deliveries folder open in File Explorer.

## Troubleshooting: eight real failures

Each entry is one screen in the app and one row in the PDF. Symptom in the user's
words, cause in one line, fix as a button.

| You see | Why it happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "It's still making the video, but I closed the app." | Closing the app stops the current step; finished steps are kept. | Open the app. Click **Continue** on the banner. Nothing already finished is done again. |
| "The preview is black, but the file plays on my phone." | Your graphics driver cannot show this preview format. The exported file is fine. | Open **Settings → Preview** and choose **Standard**. If it is still black, export and check the file. |
| "It stopped and said there isn't enough space." | A 9-file export needs room for the files plus working space. | Free 5 GB, or move the project to another drive in **Settings → Storage**, then click **Try again**. |
| "The voice sounds rushed or stops halfway." | The script is longer than the video. The app will not speed up a voice to fit. | Shorten the script, or choose **Keep my audio**. Aim for 2 words per second. |
| "The music disappeared after I moved my folder." | The track was outside the project folder, or its licence check failed. | Open the project, click **Fix music**, and choose a track from the list. |
| "Windows says 'Windows protected your PC'." | The installer is not signed, so Windows does not recognise the publisher. | Click **More info → Run anyway**, but only if you downloaded it from the official link and the checksum on the download page matches. On a work laptop, ask IT first. |
| "It can't find my clips after I moved them." | The project remembers the folder, not the new location. | Click **Locate folder** and choose the new one. The app matches files by their content, so renamed files still work. |
| "I exported 5 files and they are all the same video." | You chose 5 destinations for one version. Destinations change size and captions, not the edit. | Go to **Versions → New version** for a different edit. Keep destinations for where it is posted. |

The support address is monitored during UK office hours. The diagnostics bundle
never contains media, keys, addresses or prices; the user sees the file list
before saving it.

## Five-minute recording script

Record at 1920×1080, 30 fps, cursor highlight on, system audio off, narration
close-mic, captions burned in. Use a real listing, not a demo with fake data.
Where a step is sped up, label it on screen; never pretend a 3-minute wait is
instant.

| Time | Picture | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Finished vertical video plays, phone frame on a desk | "This is a listing video for 14 Oak Avenue. It took 12 minutes, and nobody opened an editor." |
| 0:15–0:40 | Desktop, folder of clips, facts sheet | "You need three things: the clips, the facts, and your model key. That's it." |
| 0:40–1:10 | **New listing**, address typed, folder dragged in | "Click New listing, type the address, and drag the folder in. The app copies the clips into the project, so the whole project moves as one folder later." |
| 1:10–1:40 | Facts form, price and beds filled, **These are correct** ticked | "Fill in the price, bedrooms and bathrooms. Tick this box. The app will not put a fact on screen until you confirm it." |
| 1:40–2:20 | Clip checking, thumbnails appearing, timer | "Now it checks the clips: sharpness, light, movement, which room. About 3 minutes for 10 clips. You can keep working." |
| 2:20–3:00 | Version picker, **Just listed**, **Make video**, preview | "Pick a version. If you're not sure, start with Just listed. The first preview takes about a minute." |
| 3:00–3:50 | Chat: "start with the garden"; preview card; **Apply**; **Ctrl+Z** | "To change it, just say it: start with the garden. Look at the preview, then click Apply. Changed your mind? Ctrl+Z." |
| 3:50–4:30 | Destinations, **Export**, progress, deliveries folder | "Choose where it's going. TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, the MLS. Click Export. The files are in Documents, Zeniconic, your address, deliveries." |
| 4:30–4:50 | File Explorer, three files, backup drive | "Copy the project folder to your backup drive once a week. One folder, everything inside." |
| 4:50–5:00 | End card: app icon, support address | "Your clips stay on this PC. Only small preview sheets, short voice slices and text summaries are sent for the AI. That's it." |

Do not describe codecs, bitrates or aspect ratios. Do not say "render". If a
feature is not in the build being recorded, cut the line; do not record the
roadmap.

## Printed quick-reference card

A6 landscape, 148 × 105 mm, 350 gsm, two sides, 9 pt minimum, one accent colour (brass `#B08A4A`), no screenshots.

**Front — six steps**

> **Make a video**
> 1. New listing
> 2. Add clips
> 3. Confirm the facts
> 4. Check clips
> 5. Pick a version, then Make video
> 6. Say what to change, then Apply
>
> **Export** when the preview looks right.

**Back — if something looks wrong**

> - Stuck? Reopen the app and click **Continue**.
> - Not enough space? Free 5 GB, then **Try again**.
> - Wrong edit? **Ctrl+Z**.
> - Can't find clips? **Locate folder**.
> - Five identical files? That's destinations, not versions.
>
> **Space** plays. **Ctrl+Z** undoes. **?** opens Help.
> Projects: Documents → Zeniconic
> support@zeniconic.app
> Scan for the 5-minute video.

The QR code points at `https://zeniconic.app/watch/first-video`, which redirects
to the current recording. Test it on a phone before printing; a dead QR on a
printed card is worse than no QR.

## Shipping and versioning

- The one-pager and card carry the app version in 6 pt at the foot: `1.4 · Nov 2026`.
- The PDFs are built from the same Markdown as the in-app Help, so they cannot drift.
- The recording is re-shot only when the six-step flow changes; a copy change is a
  caption edit, not a new video.
- Every document is checked against the build it ships with: button labels,
  folder names, support address.
