# Format catalog

Ten formats, ids fixed by the EDL schema. A format is the creative template; look
and hook sit on top. Lengths are house bounds on `targetDurationSec`, inside the
schema's 5–90 s. Canvas shows the shape the beats were written for; destinations
are where the format works best. A destination needing another profile re-frames
the same variant at delivery, so the crop must survive. Packaging: `platform-delivery`.

## `cinematic_hero`
**30–45 s** (target 36) · `calm` / `editorial` · landscape · YouTube, website, MLS, email, Facebook feed\
**Needs** moving establishing shot; 5–7 slow interiors; view; detail pair; logo

| Time | Beat | Picture | Overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–3.0 | Approach | Slow push or drone descent toward the building | none |
| 3.0–9.0 | Arrival | Exterior detail, door, threshold | address tag from 3.0 s |
| 9.0–19.0 | Living and kitchen | Two rooms, 5 s each, one direction of travel | none |
| 19.0–28.0 | Private + view | Bedroom, then the view at 23.5 s | none |
| 28.0–33.0 | Details | Light, texture, joinery, 2.5 s each | price from 29.0 s |
| 33.0–36.0 | Sign-off | Best wide frame, held | price, logo, CTA |

**Fails when** it opens on a hallway or a static photo; music swells before the
reveal; the first shot runs past 6 s; an overlay intrudes before 2 s; nine shots
all sit at the same distance.

## `social_hook`
**12–22 s** (target 15) · `social` · vertical-social · TikTok, Reels, Stories, Shorts\
**Needs** one frame that survives a 9:16 crop and moves; 3–5 supporting shots; a calm CTA frame

| Time | Beat | Picture | Overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.5 | Hook | The single best moving shot, already mid-action | 3–6 words, top third |
| 2.5–5.0 | Reveal | Exterior, or the room the hook promised | address + price |
| 5.0–10.0 | Flow | Three rooms, 1.5 s each, cut on beat | room label optional |
| 10.0–12.5 | Peak | The frame people screenshot | none |
| 12.5–15.0 | CTA | Calm wide or slow push | CTA card + logo |

**Fails when** the hook is a logo, black frame or address card; hook text exceeds
6 words; the reveal arrives at 6 s; the peak is a weaker shot than the hook; two
CTAs compete in the last three seconds.

## `room_walkthrough`
**35–60 s** (target 45) · `walkthrough` · vertical-social · TikTok, Reels, Shorts, website\
**Needs** continuous moving shots through 6+ rooms in walking order, 4 s minimum each, stable horizon

| Time | Beat | Picture | Overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–3.0 | Threshold | Approach to the door, or first step inside | address tag |
| 3.0–9.0 | Living | Enter and settle, widest angle first | room label |
| 9.0–15.0 | Kitchen | Continuous move, worktop to window | room label |
| 15.0–20.0 | Dining | Connect to kitchen, same travel direction | room label |
| 20.0–28.0 | Private | Two bedrooms, bathroom, hall | room label |
| 28.0–38.0 | Outdoor / view | Balcony, garden or outlook | none |
| 38.0–45.0 | CTA | Return to the best wide frame | price + CTA |

**Fails when** it backtracks (kitchen, bedroom, living again); labels appear after
the room has left; a cut lands inside a pan; there is no establishing shot; rooms
appear in filming order rather than walking order.

## `just_listed`
**15–25 s** (target 20) · `social` / `editorial` · vertical-social · TikTok, Reels, Instagram feed\
**Needs** strong exterior; 3 interiors; 1 detail; confirmed price, address and listing date

| Time | Beat | Picture | Overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.5 | Announcement | Exterior, already moving | "Just listed" + street |
| 2.5–6.0 | Reveal | Push in on facade or entrance | price |
| 6.0–13.0 | Three rooms | Living, kitchen, best bedroom, 2.3 s each | spec row from 7.0 s |
| 13.0–17.0 | Peak | Detail or view, held longer than the rest | none |
| 17.0–20.0 | CTA | Wide frame | viewing details + logo |

**Fails when** "just listed" runs on a stale listing; no listing date appears;
price is missing; it is used for a reduction or relaunch; four rooms look identical,
so the flow beat has no progression.

## `amenity_lifestyle`
**20–35 s** (target 28) · `editorial` / `social` · vertical-social · Reels, Instagram feed, Facebook feed\
**Needs** 3–5 amenity shots that belong to this building; consented human moment; sourced travel figure

| Time | Beat | Picture | Overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–3.0 | Lifestyle hook | The amenity, not the front door | 3–5 words |
| 3.0–9.0 | Amenity pair | Pool, gym or terrace — two of them, 3 s each | amenity label |
| 9.0–15.0 | Second pair | Two more, wider then closer | amenity label |
| 15.0–20.0 | Location | Street context, transport, map graphic | sourced travel time |
| 20.0–25.0 | Property reveal | Re-enter the building, best interior wide | price at 20.5 s |
| 25.0–28.0 | CTA | Held interior, one push in | CTA + logo |

**Fails when** amenity footage comes from another development; stock people are
unlicensed; "5 minutes to the station" has no source; amenities are not in the
listing; the property does not appear by 70 % of runtime.

## `price_drop`
**10–18 s** (target 12) · `social` · vertical-social · TikTok, Reels, Instagram feed\
**Needs** hero exterior; 2 interiors; recorded old price, new price, reduction date. No date, no format

| Time | Beat | Picture | Overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.5 | New price first | Hero exterior, mid-motion | "Now £795,000" |
| 2.5–5.0 | Contrast | Same or second exterior | old price struck, new price |
| 5.0–9.0 | Two rooms | Best two, 2 s each | spec row |
| 9.0–12.0 | CTA | Best wide | "Reduced 8 Nov" + CTA |

**Fails when** the old price is larger than the new; no reduction date; urgency the
record cannot support ("must sell", "bargain"); the `just_listed` caption is reused;
the drop is announced in the last second.

## `agent_intro`
**25–40 s** (target 30) · `editorial` / `calm` · feed-portrait · Reels, Instagram feed, Facebook, website\
**Needs** agent to camera with lav audio and consent; 2 b-roll shots; 1 property establishing; logo and contact

| Time | Beat | Picture | Overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–3.0 | Name and role | Agent to camera, mid-speech | name + agency |
| 3.0–10.0 | Why this area | Agent + one local b-roll | area name |
| 10.0–18.0 | One listing | Property establishing, then agent | price, no CTA yet |
| 18.0–25.0 | Proof | Testimonial card or sourced figure | source in small print |
| 25.0–30.0 | Contact | Agent still, calm background | phone, logo, CTA |

**Fails when** the agent speaks before the name appears; "top agent" has no source;
eyes are cropped by the 4:5 frame; contact lives only in the caption; b-roll
contradicts what is being said.

## `before_after`
**15–25 s** (target 18) · `social` · vertical-social · TikTok, Reels, Instagram feed\
**Needs** 2+ matched pairs: same position, lens and height; renovation consent; record of work done

| Time | Beat | Picture | Overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.5 | After, first | Finished room, moving | "After" tag |
| 2.5–4.5 | Wipe to before | Matched before, held 2 s | "Before" tag |
| 4.5–7.0 | Wipe back | After again, tighter | none |
| 7.0–13.0 | Second pair | Same grammar, faster | room label |
| 13.0–18.0 | Wide after + CTA | Best finished wide | price + CTA |

**Fails when** angles are unmatched and it reads as two rooms; the wipe is faster
than 0.4 s and cannot be read; the before shot is better lit than the after; work is
claimed that the record does not support; there are more than three pairs.

## `drone_hero`
**20–35 s** (target 24) · `calm` · landscape · YouTube, website, Facebook feed, Pinterest\
**Needs** permitted flight; 3+ distinct passes (establish, descend, orbit); clean horizon; no identifiable people

| Time | Beat | Picture | Overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–4.0 | Establishing | High wide, slow forward | area name, small |
| 4.0–9.0 | Reveal | Descent toward the property | none |
| 9.0–15.0 | Orbit | Property in context, one direction only | address tag |
| 15.0–20.0 | Setting | Neighbourhood, view, grounds | none |
| 20.0–24.0 | Sign-off | Best frame, held | price, logo, CTA |

**Fails when** the flight had no permission or crossed restricted space; the horizon
tilts; one pass is reused four times; 30 fps conformed to 24 fps judders; there is no
ground-level shot to anchor it.

## `vertical_tour`
**45–75 s** (target 60) · `walkthrough` · vertical-social · TikTok, Reels, Shorts, saved link\
**Needs** 9:16-native capture or 4K landscape with real headroom; continuous walk; 8–12 rooms; stair transitions

| Time | Beat | Picture | Overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.5 | Door hook | Hand on the door, or step through | "Come in" + street |
| 2.5–8.0 | Entry | Hall, sightline into the first room | address tag |
| 8.0–20.0 | Public | Living, kitchen, dining, 4 s each | room labels |
| 20.0–38.0 | Private | Bedrooms, bathroom, storage, 4.5 s each | room labels |
| 38.0–48.0 | Outdoor / view | Balcony, garden, outlook | none |
| 48.0–56.0 | Detail / amenity | Building amenity or two details | none |
| 56.0–60.0 | CTA | Return to the entry sightline | price + CTA |

**Fails when** the portrait crop cuts doorframes and heads; labels collide with
destination UI; it runs past 75 s where completion collapses; there is no sense of
chapter; a camera turn reveals the crew.

## Cross-format rules

1. Never exceed a maximum by more than 2 s to "fit one more room". Cut the room; the
   `duration` check fails either way.
2. Peak beat starts at 60–75 % of runtime (55–65 % at 15–20 s). `price_drop` peaks on
   the price at 0.0. Earlier makes the CTA an afterthought; later leaves no CTA.
3. `just_listed` and `price_drop` show price by 30 % of runtime. `amenity_lifestyle`
   may hold it to 70 % — the amenities are the offer there.
4. `agent_intro` and `before_after` are the only formats where a person may hold more
   than 50 % of runtime, and both need consent on file.
5. If two formats fit equally, choose the shorter: a second variant is easy, a bloated
   first cut is not.
