# Zeniconic for the agent

One page for the person whose name is on the listing. You do not operate the app;
your assistant does. This is what you are approving, what the tool will not do, and
where your responsibility starts.

## What it does

It takes the folder of footage from a listing and returns finished videos: three
different edits, each rendered in the shapes marketing needs, with an optional
price card, captions and a cover frame. The three edits are genuinely different,
not one cut repeated at three sizes.

- **Calm** for the portal and the website, slower and quieter.
- **Social** for the first three seconds on a phone, captions on.
- **Walkthrough** for room order and labels, when the layout is the selling point.

Output is 9:16 for social, 16:9 for portals and the website, and 1:1 for WhatsApp
and portal thumbnails. Feed-portrait and Pinterest shapes are `[coming]`.

The editing and rendering happen on the office laptop. Footage is never uploaded.
Only a small number of still frames, and short audio slices when speech matters, are
sent to the model that tags rooms and plans the cut. Nothing is posted anywhere.

## What it will not do

- **It will not invent a fact.** Price, address, beds and baths are confirmed by
  your assistant before export, and every caption is built from confirmed facts
  only. If the brief and the footage disagree, it stops rather than choosing.
- **It will not change a price quietly.** A price change after export leaves the
  old files untouched and marks them out of date. New exports use the new price.
- **It will not publish.** There is no upload, no caption scheduling and no
  platform connection. You get files, and marketing posts them.
- **It will not replace an editor on a luxury listing.** For drone-heavy or
  high-value work it produces coverage, a rough cut and a shot list for a human to
  finish.
- **It will not make a bad recording sound good.** A room that echoes stays a room
  that echoes. It says so in plain words, and the choice is to re-record or to use
  an AI voice.

## Turnaround

| Stage | Time |
|---|---|
| Your assistant at the keyboard | About 15 minutes |
| Three drafts to watch | Playable within a minute of planning |
| Finals, unattended | 30 to 70 minutes for nine files |
| Total, same afternoon | Under two hours, including your review |

Three approved edits across three shapes is nine files, plus a `.srt` where
captions are on and a cover `.jpg` beside each. Files are named by listing, format,
look, shape and version, and an existing file is never overwritten.

## How to ask for a change

Say one change at a time, in plain words: "start on the garden", "drop the second
bedroom", "bring the price card in earlier". Your assistant types it into
**Refine**; the app shows exactly what will change, plays a preview, and applies it
only when she presses **Apply**. The previous version is kept.

Two things do not go through that route:

- **Price, address, beds and baths** are changed on the facts screen, not in the
  conversation. The assistant will refuse them in chat, on purpose, because a
  creative edit must never move a number a buyer relies on.
- **A change after export** means a new export. The old files stay as they were,
  marked out of date.

## What it costs

- **Model credit:** about $0.20–0.60 per listing, taken from the branch's prepaid
  balance and shown before the first paid call. The listing stops at its cap.
- **Rendering:** free. It runs on the laptop and is not metered.
- **Optional upscale:** charged per file, priced in dollars before it runs, never
  applied to a batch, off by default.
- **Remotion licence:** free for a team of three or fewer; $25 per seat per month
  above that. It applies to whoever uses the app, not to you.

## Compliance is ours, not the app's

The app produces the video. It does not clear the advert.

- The **Trakheesi permit line** is an optional overlay, off by default. Marketing
  adds it where the advert needs it; the app does not gate export on it.
- **Captions and on-screen text** avoid "guaranteed", investment or income claims,
  and phrasing that implies a preferred buyer. If a claim is not in the confirmed
  facts, it does not appear.
- **Music** comes from a cleared library, and export is blocked if a track has no
  licence record.
- **You approve the facts and the final look.** The app records who approved which
  version and when, so "that is not the cut I saw" has an answer.

## Before it goes out

1. Watch all three drafts, or have your assistant play them on her laptop.
2. Check the price, the address and the bedroom count on screen.
3. Name one change if something is wrong; do not list five.
4. Send the approved folder, not a draft. Drafts are for review only.

Questions about the edit: ask your assistant first. Questions about the advert,
the permit or the platform: marketing.
