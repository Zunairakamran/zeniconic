# Quick reference

Print this and keep it beside the laptop. Full walkthrough:
[MAKE-YOUR-FIRST-VIDEO.md](MAKE-YOUR-FIRST-VIDEO.md).

## The six steps

| | Screen | Press | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Listings | **New video** | Pick the folder that holds the clips |
| 2 | Intake & facts | **Analyse** | Confirm price, address, beds, baths first |
| 3 | Analysis | **Review shots** (appears when done) | Leave it alone while it scores |
| 4 | Shot review | **Looks good, build variants** | Reject only what you would not send |
| 5 | Variants | **Approve**, then **Export** | Approve at least one; **Ask for a change** if needed |
| 6 | Formats → Export | **Continue to export**, then **Export** | Collect with **Open folder** |

**The facts gate.** Price, address, beds and baths must be confirmed before export.
A missing fact is better than a wrong one.

## The four aspect profiles

| Profile | Size | Covers | Loudness |
|---|---|---|---|
| `vertical-social` | 1080 × 1920 | TikTok, Reels, Stories, Shorts | −14 LUFS |
| `feed-portrait` | 1080 × 1350 | Instagram and Facebook feed | −14 LUFS |
| `landscape` | 1920 × 1080 | YouTube, website, MLS, email | −16 LUFS |
| `pin` | 1000 × 1500 | Pinterest | −14 LUFS |

**Formats** offers 9:16 (1080 × 1920), 16:9 (1920 × 1080) and 1:1 (1080 × 1080)
today. Feed-portrait and pin are designed but not selectable yet; they are
`[coming]` as shapes.

## Where the files land

- The folder path is printed on the **Export** screen, under *Output files*. On
  this build: `D:\Zeniconic\data\<listing-id>`.
- **Settings** › **Storage** shows the same root and how much space is left.
- Name: `<listing>_<format>_<look>_<profile>_v<N>.mp4`
- Beside it: `.srt` when captions are on, `.jpg` for the cover.
- Files are never overwritten. A repeat export gets `-2`, then `-3`.

## 30 seconds or 60

| If the video is for | Aim for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Social, first impression | 30 s | Long enough to show three rooms, short enough to finish |
| A walkthrough, a portal | 60 s | Room order and labels need the room |
| Anything under 15 s | Too short | One room, no story |
| Anything over 60 s | Only if the property is the point | Retention falls away after a minute |

Format defaults in the app: a social hook 22 s (15–30 s), a hero cut 45 s
(30–60 s), a room walkthrough 60 s (45–90 s). Stay inside the range the screen
shows; it refuses anything outside.

## Numbers worth knowing

- Analysis: about 55 s, ceiling 90 s for five minutes of 4K.
- Drafts: playable within a minute of planning.
- Finals: 30–70 minutes for nine files, unattended.
- Model spend: about $0.20–0.60 per listing, shown before the first paid call.
- Fifteen minutes at the keyboard is the target.
