# Screen recording script

A five-minute demo for Loom or OBS, aimed at an assistant who has never opened the
app and an agent who will only watch the first ninety seconds. Narration is written
to be read aloud at a normal pace. The brackets are stage directions, not lines.

## Before you record

- Window at 1440 × 900, light theme, browser tabs and chat closed.
- Use the demo listing, not a live client address. Zoom the window slightly rather
  than the capture, so text stays sharp when it is compressed.
- Turn notifications off (Windows **Focus assist**), and unplug headphones so the
  system sounds are not recorded mid-sentence.
- Have one approved variant ready so **Export** is not empty. Do not pre-run the
  export you intend to show.
- Aim for one take. Record at most two takes and upload one; edit nothing.

## The script

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | **Listings**, cursor still | "This is Zeniconic. It turns a folder of property footage into finished videos for the shapes the marketing team needs. The footage stays on this laptop and nothing is published from here." |
| 0:25–1:05 | Press **New video**, then **Add footage**, pick the folder, let the clip count appear | "One listing, one folder. I press New video, choose the folder the clips are in, and the app reads the footage locally. Twenty-three clips, four minutes of material. It copies the files, so the originals on the card are never touched." |
| 1:05–1:50 | **Intake & facts**, scroll the Facts card, edit one value (for example beds) and press **Analyse** | "This is the part that matters. Price, address, beds and baths have to be confirmed before anything can be exported. Each fact shows where it came from: the listing sheet, or me. If the sheet and a clip disagree, it stops here. A missing fact is better than a wrong one." |
| 1:50–2:35 | **Analysis**, let the stages run, point at the contact sheet | "Analysis scores every shot and tags the rooms. About a minute for this much footage. I can work on another listing, or close the app, and it picks up where it stopped. The estimate is measured on this machine, not a promise." |
| 2:35–3:10 | **Shot review**, press the **Rejected** chip, then **Looks good, build variants** | "This is my ten-second check. Rooms on the left, scores on the right, and a plain reason on every shot I would not send to an agent. Rejecting one does not delete it. Then the app builds three different edits from what is left." |
| 3:10–4:05 | **Variants**, watch the cut, **Approve** it, then **Ask for a change**, type one line, **Send**, **Preview 3 changes**, **Apply 3 changes** | "Three complete edits, not three copies. I watch each one and approve the ones I would send. If one is nearly right, I ask for one change in a sentence. It comes back as a list of exactly what will change; nothing applies until I have watched the preview and pressed Apply. The old version is kept." |
| 4:05–4:35 | **Export** on Variants, **Formats**, tick 1:1, point at the file count, **Continue to export** | "Formats is packaging, not editing. Each shape is the same approved cut fitted to that frame, and the count updates as I tick: two approved variants across three shapes is six files. Text stays inside the margins the phone will cover." |
| 4:35–5:00 | **Export**, press **Export**, let the bar move, stop before it finishes, then the done panel and **Open folder** | "One explicit step, then it writes to disk. Finals take half an hour or so and run unattended; closing the window keeps the queue. Files are named by listing, format, look, shape and version, and they are never overwritten. That is the whole job: folder in, files out." |

## If something misbehaves

- **Export is greyed out.** No variant is approved. Go back to Variants, press
  **Approve**, and say: "It will not export an edit I have not watched."
- **Analysis finishes early.** Keep the 1:50 narration and move to 2:35. Do not
  fill the gap by reading the stage names twice.
- **The export bar is slow.** Cut at 4:50, say "and it keeps writing while I do
  something else", and stop the recording. Do not wait on camera.
- **The card player is missing.** The in-card play control is `[coming]`. Open
  **Ask for a change** and use the player in **Refine**, then carry on at 3:40.
- **A dialog appears.** Stop, fix it, start again from the nearest section. Cut the
  take rather than explaining the dialog.

## After recording

1. Stop the capture before opening email, WhatsApp or the shared drive.
2. Title it `Zeniconic: folder to finished video (5 min)` and mark it unlisted.
3. Put the version number in the description, not in the narration, so the video
   does not date the moment a build changes.
4. Send it with [MAKE-YOUR-FIRST-VIDEO.md](MAKE-YOUR-FIRST-VIDEO.md) and
   [QUICK-REFERENCE.md](QUICK-REFERENCE.md), not instead of them.
