What the mark is

A bold Z with a brass play triangle set into its diagonal. The letter is the name; the triangle is what the product makes. The Z stays legible at 16 px, where the triangle resolves into a notch, and the brass accent carries the brand at every size.

The name is hers — Zeni — and the promise is the output: an ordinary walkthrough becomes something worth watching.

Clear space

x = one quarter of the Z's cap height

Keep at least x clear on every side of the lockup, where x is a quarter of the Z's cap height. Nothing sits inside that box: no tagline, no border, no crop. Measure from the outermost pixel of the Z, not from the triangle.

The mark uses brass on paper and brass on ink. At sizes below 24 px use brass text (#7A5A24) on light surfaces for contrast. No other colours are permitted.

Misuse

Don't stretch. Scale proportionally, always.
Don't add effects. No shadow, bevel or glow.
Don't rotate. The Z sits level.
Don't recolour. Brass, ink or paper only.
Don't outline. It is a solid shape, not a line icon.
Don't sit on busy footage without a scrim.
