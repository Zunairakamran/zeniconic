04

Shot review

Thirty-four shots, four columns, one reason each — enough to reject with confidence.

  • Every tile states its reason. A score without a "why" becomes anxiety, so the line is always visible.
  • Rejected shots stay in the grid, desaturated with the reason attached — nothing disappears silently.
  • Filters follow the house, not the file system: kitchen, living, bedroom. No clip names to decode.

06

Refine chat

Ask in plain language, inspect the exact diff, then apply it as a new version.

  • The preview is dark and quiet, so the grade, the price card and the cut are the only things competing.
  • The diff names timecodes. Three changes, each with the moment it touches and the runtime effect — inspectable, not magic.
  • Apply is gated on preview. Version 4 stays whole until version 5 is seen and accepted.

07

Formats

Choose the shapes to render, and see which files leave the app before committing to them.

  • Three shapes, one edit. Each shape is the approved cut reframed, not a second video and not a second approval.
  • The shape is the output. The app decides nothing about where a file goes; marketing does.
  • One safe-area rule, shown once. Vertical is the tightest case: 250 top, 340 bottom, 120 right, 60 left.
